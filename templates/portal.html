Why Custos?

Custos comes from the Latin word for guardian or keeper. The name reflects the portal's purpose: to safeguard assessment integrity, protect each student's work, and give the instructor a clear and accountable view of every examination session.

Install Custos

Installed app mode gives Custos a cleaner, browser-chrome-free exam workspace on phones and tablets.

iPhone / iPad
  1. Open Custos in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Launch Custos from the new Home Screen icon.
Android
  1. Open Custos in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Open the browser menu.
  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  4. Launch Custos from the installed app icon.
Install from your browser

Use your browser's Install or Add to Home Screen command, then launch Custos from the installed app icon.
