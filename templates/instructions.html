Monitoring, Recording & Assessment Terms

{% if assessment and assessment.security_mode != 'strict' %}

This assessment uses Standard / open-tool mode. Custos records your answers, saves, timestamps, timer state, and messages, but it does not apply fullscreen-exit penalties or permanent security locks.

{% if assessment.subject_code == 'CSEC303' %}

You may use the supplied Jupyter Case Kit and switch between Jupyter and Custos. Keep your working notebook as your laboratory working paper and submit your graded responses through Custos.

{% endif %}
Authorized ToolsUse the tools explicitly permitted by the assessment instructions. CSEC303 laboratory work may require Jupyter and the supplied evidence files.
Saved ProgressCustos stores answer progress and your current position so you can recover from a refresh or reconnect.
Academic IntegrityYour submitted answers must reflect your own investigation and reasoning even when external tools are permitted.
CommunicationUse the instructor chat for clarifications about the case files, instructions, or technical problems.
{% else %}

This assessment is conducted in a monitored environment. Custos records exam activity, including your answers and answer changes, timestamps, autosave activity, timer state, fullscreen/focus events, blocked shortcuts, right-click attempts, inactivity events, security violations, and messages sent through the instructor chat.

During the examination, your laboratory workstation may also be monitored by the instructor through Veyon. Custos itself does not request access to your camera or microphone.

Leaving the ExamAlt+Tab, changing tabs/windows, or leaving fullscreen is treated as a major security violation when detected.
Violations 1–2The exam interface is blacked out and locked for 15 seconds. The exam timer continues.
Violation 3Your attempt is permanently locked. You cannot continue answering until the instructor manually unlocks it.
Other Security EventsBlocked shortcuts, right-click attempts, navigation/reload attempts, and prolonged inactivity are logged and may be reviewed by the instructor.

Browser-based monitoring cannot detect or prevent every operating-system action. Custos uses the security signals browsers expose and preserves them as an audit trail; Veyon can provide additional workstation supervision.
