ASSESSMENT INTEGRITY NOTICE

Monitoring, Recording & Security Terms

This assessment is conducted in a monitored environment. Custos records exam activity, including your answers and answer changes, timestamps, autosave activity, timer state, fullscreen/focus events, blocked shortcuts, right-click attempts, inactivity events, security violations, and messages sent through the instructor chat.

During the examination, your laboratory workstation will also be monitored by the instructor through Veyon from the teacher's computer. Custos itself does not request access to your camera or microphone.

Leaving the Exam Alt+Tab, changing tabs/windows, or leaving fullscreen is treated as a major security violation when detected.
Violations 1–2 The exam interface is blacked out and locked for 15 seconds. The exam timer continues.
Violation 3 Your attempt is permanently locked. You cannot continue answering until the instructor manually unlocks it.
Other Security Events Blocked shortcuts, right-click attempts, navigation/reload attempts, and prolonged inactivity are logged and may be reviewed by the instructor.

Browser-based monitoring cannot detect or prevent every operating-system action. Custos uses the security signals browsers expose and preserves them as an audit trail; Veyon provides the instructor with additional workstation supervision.
